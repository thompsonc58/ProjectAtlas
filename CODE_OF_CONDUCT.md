# Project Atlas Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Project Atlas is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and
collaborative environment.

## Expected Behavior

-   Be respectful and constructive.
-   Focus on ideas, not individuals.
-   Welcome feedback.
-   Support evidence-based discussion.

## Unacceptable Behavior

-   Harassment or discrimination.
-   Personal attacks.
-   Bad-faith contributions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary
to protect the community.
