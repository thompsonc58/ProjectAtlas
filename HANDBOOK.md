# Welcome to Project Atlas

Welcome to **Project Atlas**, an open-source Curriculum Engineering
System (CES) designed to bring the discipline, rigor, and repeatability
of engineering to collegiate curriculum development.

Atlas is built on a simple belief:

> **Curriculum is not merely written---it is engineered.**

Just as software engineers build reliable systems through defined
architectures, standards, processes, quality assurance, and continuous
improvement, Atlas applies the same engineering mindset to curriculum.

The result is a repeatable methodology that enables institutions to
create high-quality, evidence-based curriculum aligned with industry
needs and student success.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Engineering Better Learning

Atlas exists to bridge the gap between higher education and professional
practice.

Curriculum should reflect the knowledge, skills, tools, and
problem-solving approaches used by today's professionals while
maintaining academic rigor and supporting institutional goals.

Atlas combines:

-   Real-world professional experience
-   Faculty expertise
-   Evidence-based instructional design
-   AI-assisted curriculum engineering
-   Continuous improvement

Together, these principles create a curriculum engineering methodology
designed specifically for colleges and universities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Guiding Philosophy

Atlas is founded on the following principles:

-   Curriculum is engineered.
-   Industry drives competency development.
-   Competencies define learning outcomes.
-   Learning outcomes drive instruction.
-   Assessments collect evidence.
-   Rubrics measure competency.
-   Evidence informs continuous improvement.
-   Faculty remain the final authority over curriculum decisions.
-   AI augments engineering---it does not replace human expertise.
