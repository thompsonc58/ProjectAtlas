# About This Handbook

The **Atlas Handbook** is the foundational publication of the **Atlas Curriculum Engineering Framework (ACEF)**. It establishes the philosophy, terminology, methodology, and engineering practices that define the emerging discipline of **Curriculum Engineering**.

Unlike traditional curriculum development guides, this handbook approaches education from an engineering perspective. It presents curriculum as a system that can be intentionally designed, systematically evaluated, continuously improved, and supported through governance, standards, and automation.

The Atlas Handbook serves as the authoritative reference for educators, curriculum developers, instructional designers, academic leaders, industry partners, researchers, and software developers seeking to apply engineering principles to educational systems.

This first edition introduces the conceptual foundations upon which the Atlas Framework is built.

---

# Vision

To establish **Curriculum Engineering** as a recognized engineering discipline that transforms educational design into a systematic, measurable, and continuously improving practice.

---

# Mission

The Atlas Framework empowers educators and institutions to engineer high-quality learning experiences through structured methodologies, standardized practices, governance, and continuous improvement.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this handbook is to:

- Define Curriculum Engineering as an engineering discipline.
- Establish the philosophy of the Atlas Framework.
- Introduce the Atlas Method.
- Describe the Curriculum Engineering Lifecycle.
- Provide a common vocabulary for Atlas practitioners.
- Serve as the conceptual foundation for all Atlas standards, specifications, templates, and software.

This handbook focuses on concepts rather than implementation details. Technical specifications are documented separately within the Atlas Framework.

---

# Guiding Principle

> **Curriculum deserves to be engineered.**

Educational systems should be designed with the same rigor, traceability, quality assurance, and continuous improvement practices that have transformed disciplines such as software engineering, systems engineering, and manufacturing.
