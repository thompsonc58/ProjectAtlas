# ATLAS-PRC-001 --- Curriculum Engineering Lifecycle Framework

## Purpose

The Curriculum Engineering Lifecycle defines the repeatable process used
by Atlas to engineer, validate, release, deliver, and continuously
improve collegiate curriculum.

## Lifecycle Overview

``` text
Requirements
      ↓
Architecture
      ↓
Design
      ↓
Verification
      ↓
Release
      ↓
Delivery
      ↓
Continuous Improvement
      └──────────────► Requirements
```

## Phase 1 --- Requirements

**Purpose:** Identify educational needs and required competencies.

**Inputs** - Industry needs - Accreditation - Employer feedback -
Faculty recommendations - Technology trends

**Outputs** - Requirements Specification - Competency Matrix

## Phase 2 --- Architecture

Engineer curriculum structure:

-   Student Learning Outcomes
-   Weekly Modules
-   Lessons
-   Assessment strategy

## Phase 3 --- Design

Develop:

-   Lessons
-   Learning activities
-   Assessments
-   Rubrics
-   Questions

## Phase 4 --- Verification

Validate:

-   Standards compliance
-   Competency alignment
-   Accessibility
-   Technical quality

Quality gates are recommended and may be adapted by the institution.

## Phase 5 --- Release

-   Assign version
-   Publish curriculum
-   Produce release notes
-   Archive prior version

## Phase 6 --- Delivery

Faculty deliver instruction while collecting evidence of learning.

## Phase 7 --- Continuous Improvement

Improvement is triggered by:

-   Student performance
-   Accreditation changes
-   Technology evolution
-   Faculty requests
-   Annual review (where required)

Outputs include updated requirements and change requests for the next
lifecycle.

## Roles

### Faculty Curriculum Engineer

Owns curriculum engineering and final approval.

### Subject Matter Expert

Validates technical and industry accuracy.
