# Purpose

This document establishes the long-term direction of Project Atlas by
defining its vision and mission. Together, these statements provide the
foundation for every engineering decision, curriculum artifact, and
continuous improvement effort within the Atlas framework.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Vision

**To create an adaptive, reusable, and industry-aligned curriculum
engineering framework that promotes exceptional student learning while
empowering instructors through effective, efficient, and sustainable
educational resources.**

## Vision Explained

Project Atlas envisions a future where curriculum is engineered with the
same discipline, precision, and commitment to quality found in
professional software development.

Educational resources should not be static documents that are recreated
each semester. Instead, they should be living engineering artifacts that
evolve through documentation, collaboration, version control, assessment
analytics, and continuous improvement.

Atlas seeks to establish a framework where:

-   Students receive consistent, high-quality learning experiences.
-   Instructors spend more time mentoring students and less time
    rebuilding course materials.
-   Educational resources remain current with industry expectations.
-   Curriculum becomes a sustainable institutional asset rather than
    individual instructor knowledge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Mission

**To engineer high-quality, version-controlled educational resources
using software engineering principles, evidence-based instructional
design, and continuous improvement practices that prepare students for
successful careers in computing.**

## Mission Explained

Project Atlas fulfills its mission by applying engineering practices
throughout the curriculum lifecycle.

These practices include:

-   Intentional curriculum planning
-   Learning outcome alignment
-   Standards-driven assessment design
-   Professional documentation
-   Repository management
-   Version control
-   Quality assurance and review
-   Continuous refinement through assessment data

The goal is to create educational resources that are accurate, reusable,
maintainable, measurable, and professionally engineered.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Strategic Objectives

## Student Success

Develop curriculum that strengthens technical knowledge, problem-solving
ability, professional communication, and career readiness.

## Instructor Effectiveness

Provide reusable resources that reduce duplicated effort while improving
instructional consistency.

## Industry Alignment

Ensure curriculum reflects current technologies, tools, workflows, and
professional software engineering practices.

## Curriculum Sustainability

Create educational resources that remain valuable beyond a single
semester or instructor through documentation, version control, and
structured maintenance.

## Continuous Improvement

Use evidence, assessment results, instructor feedback, and industry
input to guide ongoing refinement of curriculum.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Guiding Philosophy

> **Curriculum deserves the same engineering discipline as the software
> we teach students to build.**

Every educational artifact should be:

-   Designed intentionally
-   Documented thoroughly
-   Reviewed critically
-   Version controlled
-   Continuously improved

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Decision Filter

Before introducing a new process, resource, or educational artifact,
ask:

1.  Does this improve student learning?
2.  Does this support instructors?
3.  Does this align with industry expectations?
4.  Can it be reused and maintained?
5.  Does it uphold Atlas Quality?

If the answer to any question is **No**, revise before implementation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Looking Forward

Project Atlas begins as an internal curriculum engineering initiative
supporting software development education.

As the framework matures, Atlas is intended to become a scalable model
that can support additional computing disciplines, foster instructor
collaboration, and contribute reusable educational resources to the
broader academic community.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Project Atlas is more than a repository.

It is a commitment to engineering curriculum with purpose,

artifact created today becomes a stronger foundation for tomorrow's
learners.
