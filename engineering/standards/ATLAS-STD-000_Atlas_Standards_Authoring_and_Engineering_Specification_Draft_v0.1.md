## 1. Scope
This standard establishes the governing rules, authoring conventions, engineering principles, and documentation requirements for all standards published within the Atlas Curriculum Engineering Body of Knowledge (CEBoK). All Atlas standards shall conform to this standard unless an approved exception is documented.

## 2. Normative References

## 5. Atlas Engineering Pattern (AEP)
Every Atlas engineering artifact shall follow this structure:
- Purpose
- Scope
- Normative References
- Definitions
- Requirements
- Inputs
- Outputs
- Interfaces
- Quality Requirements
- Traceability
- Verification
- Validation
- Conformance
- Revision History

## 6. Requirement Specification Rules
Every normative requirement should include:
- Identifier
- Statement
- Rationale
- Source
- Verification Method
- Traceability

## 8. Normative Language
- SHALL — mandatory
- SHOULD — recommended
- MAY — optional
- MUST NOT — prohibited

## 9. Traceability Requirements
Every engineering artifact shall maintain bidirectional traceability from institutional mission through assessment evidence.

## 10. Metadata Requirements
Each artifact shall include:
- Title
- Standard Identifier
- Version
- Status
- Author
- Owner
- Approval Authority
- Effective Date
- Review Date
- Revision History

## 11. Engineering Artifact Lifecycle
Mission → Requirements → Architecture → Specifications → Implementation → Verification → Validation → Maintenance → Continuous Improvement

## 12. Document Structure
1. Scope
2. References
3. Definitions
4. Requirements
5. Engineering Guidance
6. Conformance
7. Appendices

## 13. Versioning and Change Control
Versioning should follow semantic versioning where practical. Each revision shall document version, date, author, summary of changes, and approval.

## 14. Conformance
A standard conforms to ATLAS-STD-000 when it:
- follows the Atlas Engineering Pattern;
- uses approved requirement syntax;
- includes required metadata;
- supports traceability;
- defines conformance requirements;
- maintains revision history.
