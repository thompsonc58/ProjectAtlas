# Atlas Core Values

> **The principles that guide every decision, every artifact, and every
> contribution to Project Atlas.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Purpose

Project Atlas is built on a foundation of enduring values that shape how
curriculum is engineered, reviewed, maintained, and improved.

These values are not aspirations---they are commitments. Every
contributor is expected to uphold them in both action and
decision-making.

Together, they define the culture of Atlas.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Excellence

We pursue excellence in every artifact we create.

Quality is not determined by deadlines or convenience. It is achieved
through thoughtful design, careful review, and continuous refinement.

Every lesson, assessment, rubric, project, and document should reflect
the highest professional standards.

**We do not settle for "good enough."**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Integrity

We act with honesty, transparency, and professionalism.

We acknowledge mistakes.

We welcome constructive feedback.

We make decisions based on evidence rather than preference.

Integrity builds trust, and trust is the foundation of meaningful
collaboration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Continuous Improvement

Every release is an opportunity to learn.

Curriculum evolves alongside technology, industry, and student needs.

We actively seek feedback, measure outcomes, and refine our work based
on evidence.

Improvement is our default mindset.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. Student-Centered Design

Students are the reason Atlas exists.

Every engineering decision should ultimately improve the learning
experience.

We design curriculum that is accessible, engaging, relevant, and
challenging while preparing learners for success beyond the classroom.

When faced with competing priorities, we choose the path that best
serves students.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. Engineering Discipline

Curriculum deserves the same engineering rigor applied to professional
software.

We plan before we build.

We document before we forget.

We review before we release.

We improve before we repeat.

Discipline creates consistency.

Consistency creates quality.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Collaboration

Great curriculum is built together.

We value respectful discussion, diverse perspectives, and shared
expertise.

Constructive feedback strengthens ideas.

Knowledge shared is knowledge multiplied.

Atlas succeeds because its contributors succeed together.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 7. Stewardship

Every contributor is a steward of Atlas.

We inherit the work of those who came before us, and we leave it better
than we found it.

Our responsibility extends beyond individual courses and semesters.

We build with future educators and future students in mind.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 8. Innovation

Education should evolve alongside the professions it serves.

We embrace new ideas, emerging technologies, and evidence-based
practices that improve learning.

Innovation is encouraged, but it must always be purposeful, measurable,
and aligned with educational outcomes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 9. Sustainability

Great curriculum should endure.

We build reusable, maintainable, well-documented artifacts that can
evolve over time without unnecessary duplication or complexity.

Every improvement should strengthen the long-term health of Atlas.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 10. Lifelong Learning

The best educators never stop learning.

We remain curious.

We embrace change.

We seek new knowledge.

We continually develop our skills so we can better serve our students
and our profession.

Learning is both our mission and our practice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Our Commitment

These values shape every decision made within Project Atlas.

They influence how we design, collaborate, review, release, and improve
curriculum.

When faced with uncertainty, these values provide our direction.

When faced with challenges, they provide our resolve.

When faced with opportunities, they inspire us to build something worthy
of the learners we serve.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Atlas Commitment

As contributors to Project Atlas, we commit ourselves to these values
and to the responsibility of engineering curriculum that reflects
professionalism, purpose, and continuous improvement.

Every artifact should embody these principles.

Every release should strengthen this foundation.

Every contribution should leave Atlas better than it was before.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
