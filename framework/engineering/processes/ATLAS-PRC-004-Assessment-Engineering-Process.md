# Assessment Engineering Process

## Purpose

Engineer assessments that accurately measure competencies and learning
outcomes.

## Scope

This process applies to curriculum engineering activities within Project
Atlas.

## Inputs

-   Competencies
-   Student Learning Outcomes
-   Lesson Objectives

## Outputs

-   Assessment Blueprint
-   Rubrics
-   Question Bank

## Roles and Responsibilities

-   Faculty Curriculum Engineer
-   Subject Matter Expert
-   Academic Reviewer
-   AI Engineering Assistant (support role)

## Entry Criteria

-   Required inputs are available.
-   Stakeholders have identified the need for this process.

## Workflow

``` text
Competency
↓
Learning Outcome
↓
Assessment Strategy
↓
Question Engineering
↓
Rubric Engineering
↓
Validation
```

## Exit Criteria

-   Outputs are reviewed and approved.
-   Traceability is maintained.

## Quality Gates

-   Standards compliance
-   Traceability verification
-   Peer review
-   Approval completed

## Metrics

-   Process completion rate
-   Review turnaround time
-   Traceability coverage
-   Standards compliance
